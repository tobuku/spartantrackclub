About Us

One of the Leading Track Clubs on Oahu

Spartan Track Club Hawaii is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to promoting and developing track and field for athletes of all ages across Hawaii. Since our establishment in 2018, we have produced nationally ranked athletes in a variety of events.

We provide athletes of all abilities with the opportunity to enhance their speed, technique, and overall athletic performance. With a focus on building resilience, discipline, and teamwork, we strive to foster a supportive environment that inspires a lifelong passion for athletics.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide athletes of all abilities with the opportunity to enhance their speed, technique, and overall athletic performance. With a focus on building resilience, discipline, and teamwork, we strive to foster a supportive environment that inspires a lifelong passion for athletics.

What We Offer

Training Programs

We work with athletes at every level. Training is tailored to the athlete's age, ability, event group, and long-term development needs.

⚡

Sprints & Hurdles

Speed development, acceleration mechanics, block starts, hurdle technique, and race execution for the 100m through 400m hurdles.

⇧

Jumps

Technical training for long jump, triple jump, high jump, and pole vault. Approach work, takeoff mechanics, and flight technique.

⚫

Throws

Shot put, discus, javelin, and hammer throw instruction focusing on rotational and glide technique, power development, and release mechanics.

☰

Distance & Cross Country

Endurance training for the 800m through 5000m and cross country. Pacing, aerobic development, and race strategy for middle and long distance athletes.

★

Multi-Events

Decathlon and heptathlon preparation combining sprints, jumps, throws, and endurance under the guidance of nationally recognized multi-event coaching.

▶

Speed Development

Wicket sprints, top-end speed sessions, and acceleration training. Biomechanical analysis and data-driven coaching for peak performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

We welcome athletes ages 5 and up, including first-time youth athletes, elementary school athletes, middle school athletes, high school athletes, and competitive club athletes. Post-collegiate and masters training may be available on a limited basis with coach approval.
Complete the athlete registration form on our website, pay the season fee, register for USATF membership, complete USATF age verification if competing, and make sure your USATF membership is connected to Spartan Track Club Hawaii.
Yes. USATF membership is required to officially be part of Spartan Track Club Hawaii and to compete in USATF-sanctioned meets. Membership is valid for one year. When registering, select Spartan Track Club Hawaii as your club.
Age verification is required for USATF competition and is usually completed one time once the athlete's documents are approved. This ensures fair competition in the correct age divisions.

Our main practice location is McKinley High School in Honolulu. Specific days for throws, jumps, hurdles, and specialty events may vary based on coach availability, facility access, and the competition calendar.
